## What's changed

- Carry the Bluetooth receipt timestamp through Android and Dolby head tracking. Short motion-report gaps, seeks, and stream changes recover without throwing away a valid calibration.
- Add Spatial Audio and Head Tracking to LibrePods' quick panel. The controls share the app's saved choices across the supported spatial routes.
- Stabilize AAC-ELD/PCM32 across reconnects and call transitions. Codec overrides are scoped to the current connection so a stale preference cannot replace the active route.
- Keep Game Mode's tile steady while native stream state catches up, and avoid applying stale companion or ear-detection work to a new connection.

## Dolby and Plex

The separate Dolby Headphone Preview 10 module fixes Plex playback when its E-AC-3 conversion carries the extra surround channels. On the tested Pixel 10 Pro XL, *Fall* played in 7.1 with Android head tracking active.

AirPods head tracking through LibrePods is part of the opt-in debug preview. Tidal Atmos tracking also requires its matching Dolby module and separately built native control libraries. The standard release APK includes the AAC-ELD and connection fixes.
