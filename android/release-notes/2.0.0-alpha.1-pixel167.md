## Head tracking

- When several AirPods motion reports arrive in one Bluetooth read, LibrePods processes them in order and sends the newest valid pose to Android spatial audio and the companion motion interface.
- A briefly full pose pipe skips the blocked update and resumes with fresh motion. A closed pipe or a reader that stays blocked still ends the tracking session.
- LibrePods reuses the saved calibration between readiness checks and picks up a changed calibration when it is saved.
