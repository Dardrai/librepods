## What's changed

- **Choose your quick controls in LibrePods.** Open Settings → Quick Settings to
  select the controls you want. Choices save immediately and survive reopening
  the app. The quick panel keeps its clean layout, with no editing controls.
- **Hide unsupported options automatically.** AirPods model and connection support
  determine which selected controls appear. Protected hearing controls on native
  Bluetooth require the active Apple-identity hook; dongles use their own
  capabilities. Game Mode requires confirmed native backend support.
- **Keep the layout compact.** Remaining tiles fill the available rows without
  gaps. Hiding all toggles leaves the listening-mode rail and Close button.
- **Keep support separate from loading.** Supported controls stay visible during
  temporary reads and busy states. Existing hearing modes retain their guarded
  Turn Off recovery path.
- **Follow hook and connection changes.** Update the open panel when legacy
  Bluetooth hook scope changes, and observe the replacement Game Mode controller
  after reconnecting the same AirPods.
- **Keep taps attached to their controls.** A tile that moves during a press
  cancels that gesture instead of applying it to a different control.

Visibility choices only affect the interface: they do not change AirPods settings,
request root access or reconnect audio. The native AAC-ELD/PCM32 encoder and volume
behavior are unchanged. Includes Pixel128's handover notification improvements.

## Validation

Regression checks cover saved visibility choices, app-settings entry and reopening,
panel reflow, root/module and model support, native Game Mode capability, clinical
recovery and dongle independence. Light and dark chooser/panel fixtures are checked
alongside Android lint and assembly. Live device listening was not repeated.
