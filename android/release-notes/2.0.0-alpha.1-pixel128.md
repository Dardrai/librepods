## What's changed

- **Show where your AirPods moved.** Display the existing island notification when
  another connected host sends a handover notice. Use its Bluetooth name when
  provided, or show "another device" when it is absent. Reverse-handover notices
  keep the existing reconnect instruction.
- **Read and send the correct handover format.** Handle the relay's leading byte
  before decoding its dictionary, and write the sender's score bytes in sequence.
  Reject malformed, truncated and unknown-host messages. These notifications do
  not initiate a connection, change ownership or alter audio routing.
- **Remove the Discord invite** from Settings, its translations and the related
  onboarding privacy text. Email and GitHub contact options remain available.

Includes the Pixel127 reconnect, call-profile and verified-renaming fixes.
The native AAC-ELD/PCM32 encoder and volume behavior are unchanged.

## Validation

Regression checks cover the production handover packet, its declared length and
prefix, named and unnamed hosts, invalid messages and unchanged ownership handling.
The Android CI build runs the complete unit suite, lint, native audio checks and
signed codec-package verification before publishing.

Live handover between physical devices was not repeated for this release.
