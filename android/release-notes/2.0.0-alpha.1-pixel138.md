## What's changed

- **Request faster Pro 3 motion.** The head-tracking preview temporarily requests
  a 5 ms sensor interval, targeting 200 fresh reports per second. This does not
  change the saved sensor preference or another reader's ownership. A failed or
  stalled fast request falls back to the working 20 ms interval for that connection.
- **Forward fresh measurements sooner.** Motion keeps its original arrival time
  through the app and root provider. Old buffered samples cannot masquerade as
  fresh motion, and the sensor-output worker no longer waits on system audio
  queries. Its first fresh report is immediate; later reports follow Android's
  requested cadence without accumulating polling delay.
- **Measure actual delivery.** Cached diagnostic counters distinguish sensor
  timestamps, app arrival, fresh poses, repeated Android reports and host
  forwarding age. They contain no pose history and create no per-frame logs.

Head tracking remains a **DEBUG preview**. A real Pro 3 calibration and spatial
playback were confirmed on Pixel137; this build preserves that saved calibration.
The 5 ms request is a target, not a claim that 200 Hz has been measured on Pro 3.
Android's reporting interval remains under Android's control. Host timing counters
do not measure acoustic motion-to-sound latency.

No AAC-ELD encoder, PCM32 format, audio buffer, volume curve, call routing or
Bluetooth connection setting is changed. Fixed spatial audio remains available
in the regular release APK. Dolby Atmos work is separate.

## Validation

Regression checks cover temporary rate ownership and restoration, failed startup,
transport-specific limits, preserved source timestamps, stale data, fallback,
native choice and cleanup, route-cache expiry, periodic cadence and timing
counters. CI also runs Android lint, signed full AAC-ELD/PCM32 package verification
and native audio tests.

Actual Pro 3 report rate, faster motion response and stability under active spatial
playback still require the hardware comparison.
