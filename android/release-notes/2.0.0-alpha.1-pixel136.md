## What's changed

- **Guided head-tracking setup inside LibrePods.** The DEBUG preview now opens
  from AirPods → Audio → Head Tracking. Read the instructions and tap Start when
  ready; opening the screen or returning to the app never starts a capture.
- **Move at a comfortable pace.** Setup gives you three seconds to face forward,
  five seconds to lift your chin slowly and hold, and five seconds to return to
  level. Distinct vibration cues mark the two movements; countdown updates stay
  silent and respect Android's haptic settings.
- **Readable around the camera and system bars.** Setup uses LibrePods' normal
  screen layout, with scrollable content for smaller screens and enlarged text.
  The old raw-capture screen also respects the camera cutout.
- **Clear retries and cancellation.** Instructions explain whether to hold still,
  lift your chin farther or return to your starting position. Back, Cancel or
  leaving the app stops the attempt and releases only its own motion stream.
  A successful calibration is kept separate from a completed head-tracking check.
- **Better bounded diagnostics.** Explicit DEBUG attempts save a private,
  size-limited calibration trace so failures can be investigated without asking
  you to repeat the same movement blindly. No microphone or audio is recorded.

Head tracking remains a guided preview while real-motion rendering is validated.
The setup entry is shown only for the measured Pro3 format on phones supporting
native spatial audio. It does not enable a permanent listening mode.

The working fixed-spatial switch, AAC-ELD/PCM32 playback, volume curve, Game Mode
and audio buffers are retained. No Bluetooth restart is required for this update.

## Validation

Focused checks cover slow movement at 100 Hz, each timed instruction, calibration
rejection reasons, private trace bounds, camera insets, explicit Start, haptic
cue timing, cancellation and no replay after resume. Layout checks cover both
appearances, narrow screens and enlarged text. CI validates Android lint and the
complete signed AAC-ELD/PCM32 package before publishing.
