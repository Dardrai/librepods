## What's changed

- **Pairing cards follow your theme.** Light, Dark and System use matching card,
  text, button and battery colours. Dark cards use matching animation and poster
  assets instead of showing a white rectangle behind the AirPods.
- **Update an open card when the theme changes.** Refresh its colours and artwork
  without dismissing the invitation or restarting the pairing operation. Keep its
  current stage, actions, battery readings and timeout.
- **Keep dark buttons readable while pressed.** The lighter action blue retains
  readable contrast against the full touch ripple as well as the resting card.
- **Report failed artwork regeneration.** The artwork tool fails on decoder or
  encoder errors and invalid output instead of treating a broken clip as success.

Includes Pixel129's configurable quick controls in LibrePods Settings → Quick
Settings. The quick panel itself remains free of editing controls, and unsupported
options are hidden automatically. AAC-ELD/PCM32 and volume behavior are unchanged.

## Validation

Regression checks cover app/system theme combinations, live theme changes during
pairing, artwork-player cleanup, pressed contrast in both themes and artwork-tool
failure handling. Android CI runs the full unit suite, lint, native audio checks
and signed codec-package verification before publishing.
