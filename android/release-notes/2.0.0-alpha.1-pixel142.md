## What's changed

- **Request 100 Hz head motion.** The spatial source now requests a temporary
  10 ms interval, matching Android's 100 Hz HID cadence. This gives more delivery
  margin within the existing 20 ms host-age prediction limit than the previous
  20 ms fallback, while reducing load from the previous 5 ms preferred request.
  The working 20 ms fallback remains available if the preferred stream stalls.
- **Keep independent sensors independent.** Discovering or remapping only the
  heart-rate sensor no longer resets an unchanged motion stream. Real motion
  remaps, descriptor-sequence restarts and conflicting legacy service assignments
  still retire the affected source safely.
- **Reuse the calibrated source for other renderers.** The guarded motion stream
  is now independent of Android's spatial reporting controller. The Dolby work
  can consume the same conversion, temporary source ownership and freshness
  checks without adding another AirPods parser or changing saved calibration.
  This change alone does not enable Atmos head tracking.

AAC-ELD/PCM32, audio buffers, volume, calls, saved calibration and native spatial
preferences are preserved. The approved Pixel-wide prediction profile is separate
from the APK and remains unchanged. Head tracking is still a DEBUG preview.

## Validation and remaining checks

Tests cover independent motion/heart discovery, real descriptor restarts,
temporary sampling ownership, 100 Hz source timestamps, fallback and cleanup.
HID coverage checks include delivery phases, jitter, bursts and missing-source
gaps; they preserve original timestamps and the 20 ms prediction bound. CI also
runs full Android tests, lint, native audio checks and signed codec-package checks.

Pixel141's remaining startup interruption was already visible before app
collection. The mapping bug can cause a restart, but the retained timing counters
do not establish that it caused that particular capture. Stable 100 Hz delivery
and cold-start behavior require measurement with this build; a requested rate is
not a measured rate. Dolby's physical pose direction still needs its own check.
